# Key Risks

Potential risks include:
- low audit completion rates
- weak trust in optimization recommendations
- insufficient differentiation from generic AI tooling blogs

To reduce these risks:
- recommendation quality must remain credible
- savings estimates must feel realistic
- UX should remain fast and highly shareable

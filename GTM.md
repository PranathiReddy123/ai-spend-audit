# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target User

The primary target user is:
- engineering managers
- startup founders
- technical team leads

at:
- seed to Series B startups
- AI-heavy engineering teams
- remote-first product companies

These users are highly sensitive to tooling costs because AI subscriptions scale rapidly as teams grow.

The ideal user typically manages:
- 5–50 developers
- multiple AI subscriptions
- growing operational expenses

---

# User Intent Moments

Users are most likely to want this tool when:
- reviewing monthly SaaS expenses
- evaluating engineering budgets
- comparing AI tooling costs
- scaling engineering teams
- discussing operational efficiency internally

Typical searches:
- "reduce AI tooling costs"
- "ChatGPT Team vs Enterprise pricing"
- "best AI coding tools for startups"
- "Cursor vs Copilot pricing"
- "AI SaaS cost optimization"

# First 100 Users Strategy

The first 100 users could be acquired through:
- posting comparison breakdowns on X/Twitter
- publishing AI tooling cost calculators on Reddit
- sharing engineering spend breakdowns on Indie Hackers
- launching on Product Hunt
- posting startup cost optimization threads
- direct outreach to startup founders in public communities

The key strategy is making the tool highly shareable and screenshot-friendly.

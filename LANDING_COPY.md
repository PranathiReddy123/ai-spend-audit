# FAQ

## 1. Is this audit free?

Yes. The audit is completely free and designed to help startups better understand their AI tooling costs.

---

## 2. Do I need to create an account?

No login is required to generate an audit.

---

## 3. Which AI tools are supported?

The MVP currently supports major AI tooling platforms including ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, and Perplexity.

---

## 4. How are savings estimates calculated?

Savings are estimated using plan pricing comparisons, seat counts, and optimization rules based on usage scenarios.

---

## 5. Is my company information stored publicly?

No. Sensitive company information is excluded from public-facing audit results.
